## Findings
1. **Tracking metric is correct (verified independently).** I rewrote the metric with separate code (nearest-earlier fk sample, own slerp / lag search,
   thresholds read from `campaign.json` `criteria`) and ran it on the session logs of all 43 feasible demos.
   - The min-5 s-window fraction matches the campaign **exactly** for every demo (max abs diff 0.0).
   - Hand-checked, passing: d16 1.000 (p95 6.3 mm / 2.05 deg).
   - Hand-checked, failing: d0 0.000 (p95 26.8 mm / 7.8 deg; commanded vs fk anchor differ by 12.7 mm, bridge safe-cmd p95 30.7 mm) and d12 0.484 (no clamp, p95 10.6 mm / 3.2 deg).
   - Time alignment uses the tap `reply_replay_frame` + `abs_timestamps` + lookahead 0.1 s, plus a best global lag in [0, 0.3] s (0.03-0.12 s found).
   - The anchor is the commanded TCP at the first scheduled action, which is the task's `anchor: target`. It is not fk[0], which is 12.6 mm off when the bridge clamps the start pose.
   - Thresholds: `track_pos_m` 0.01, `track_rot_deg` 3 and `ok_fraction_min` 0.9 come from `criteria.stage1b` (`Campaign.__init__` self.crit, passed into `collect` / `track_metric`; none hard-coded). `WINDOW_S` 5 is a module constant, as in the spec.
   - The metric is strict: the **minimum over all 5 s windows** must be at least 0.9. Under the looser whole-run reading, 14/43 would pass (33 %). The finding holds either way.
2. **Cause classes.**
   - safety_clamp is attributed from the bridge `SafetyFilter` counters (cone/box growth in the worst window +-1 s), trace clamp flags, or safe-cmd deviation > 5 mm.
   - 26 of 28 safety_clamp rows have a large deviation or cone fraction of 11-63 %.
   - d23 and d35 (track 0.88 / 0.48) have safe-cmd p95 = 0.0 mm and only 5 % / 4 % cone updates. "Clamp present in window, with priority over overshoot" over-labels them, and d33 / d30 / d31 are marginal. Totals are unchanged, since all of them fail anyway.
   - "overshoot" is a residual class (no clamp, pad_table or hand flag), not a measured overshoot.
   - Reflexes (8, from bridge events) and pad_table (onset counts from `sim` contacts) are separate counters. d10 spot-check: reflex at hand closing, gap goes negative, eraser gone, consistent with "squeezed out".
   - d25 and d43 are labelled "pad-table cartesian" in the worklog, but the contacts at the reflex are the grip (30 N) plus eraser-table. d43 is borderline (20.1 vs 20.0 N). The label is loose.
3. **Overrides.**

Those rows passed the default check (start_err below 15 mm or inside the 5 cm default), so the results are unaffected. Record both next time.
   - The latency override must be dropped, and the campaign rerun, once the A7 sender fix lands.
   - **YAML `tcp_x_along_hand`.** Both tooling YAMLs say `"+y"` with the comment "camera sits on TCP -y". That comment is true for either value, because "camera on TCP -y" is in the TCP frame.
     The flag flips the TCP frame, and so the **physical** side. I built the model with each value: `F_T_cam_main` = (+82.7, -82.7) mm for `+y` and (-82.7, +82.7) mm for `-y` (flange frame).
     So the YAML's `+y` and Stage 1's recommended `-y` are **opposite physical sides** of the hand. Neither is "hand -y", which is a finger. Adopting Stage 1's `-y` means changing the YAML value and moving the phone to the other side. The comment should be reworded, and STATUS.md "phone on -y" should say "`tcp_x_along_hand=-y`". The stage1 campaign ran with `-y` (verified in `cmd.txt`, `F_T_TCP` = Rz(-135 deg)).
